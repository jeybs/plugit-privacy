Frequently Asked Questions

What is PlugIt?

PlugIt is a macOS app that reacts to supported power and device events with customizable sounds and experiences.

Why isn't a sound playing?

Make sure the reaction is enabled inside PlugIt and that your Mac's volume is turned up. You can also try selecting a different sound and triggering the event again.

Do I need an account?

No. PlugIt does not require you to create an account to use the app.

Can I use PlugIt on another Mac?

If you purchased PlugIt Pro using your Apple Account, you can download PlugIt on another compatible Mac using the same Apple Account and restore your purchase when needed.

Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased PlugIt Pro but the app is not recognizing your purchase:

  1. Make sure you are signed in to the Mac App Store using the same Apple Account that was used for the original purchase.
  2. Open PlugIt.
  3. Open the PlugIt Pro or purchase screen.
  4. Click Restore Purchases.

Your purchase will be checked through Apple's App Store services. You will not be charged again for restoring an existing purchase.

Report an Issue

If you encounter a problem, please include as much information as possible when contacting support, such as:

  • Your macOS version
  • Your Mac model, if relevant
  • What you were doing when the issue occurred
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Screenshots, if applicable
