Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PlugIt ("PlugIt", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the PlugIt application.

PlugIt is designed with privacy in mind. The application primarily operates locally on your device and does not require you to create an account.

1. Information We Collect

PlugIt does not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information to our own servers.

PlugIt does not require information such as your:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Location
  • Browsing history

2. Device Events

PlugIt may monitor supported device events, such as charger, power, battery, USB, or similar hardware connection events, in order to provide the application's sound reaction features.

These events are processed locally on your device and are not sent to our servers.

3. Local App Data

PlugIt may store preferences and settings locally on your device, including settings such as:

  • Selected reaction sounds
  • Sound preferences
  • Volume preferences
  • Enabled or disabled reactions
  • Application configuration

This information is used only to provide and remember your preferred PlugIt configuration.

4. Purchases and PlugIt Pro

PlugIt may offer optional paid features or a PlugIt Pro upgrade through Apple's App Store and StoreKit services.

Payments are processed by Apple. PlugIt does not receive or store your credit card number, bank information, or other payment credentials.

Apple may process information relating to your purchase according to Apple's own privacy policy and applicable App Store terms.

5. Analytics and Tracking

PlugIt does not use your information for advertising or cross-application tracking.

PlugIt does not sell your personal information to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

6. Third-Party Services

PlugIt may rely on services provided by Apple that are necessary for application distribution, purchases, operating system functionality, and related platform services.

Information processed directly by Apple is governed by Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

7. Children's Privacy

PlugIt does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because PlugIt does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information, we do not knowingly maintain personal information belonging to children.

8. Data Security

Because PlugIt is designed to process its primary functionality locally on your device, the amount of information transmitted outside your device is minimized.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and your Apple account.

9. International Users

PlugIt may be available to users in multiple countries and regions. Regardless of where you use PlugIt, the application's core functionality is designed to process application preferences and device events locally whenever possible.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if PlugIt's functionality, services, or legal requirements change.

When this policy is updated, the revised effective date will be displayed at the top of this page.
